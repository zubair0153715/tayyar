About the deadline

Islamabad is set to become Pakistan's first fully cashless city by 31 December 2026. After 30 November 2026 no cash payment will be accepted in any ICT administration / CDA department. TAYYAR gets merchants ready before the deadline: documents, Raast QR, digital catalog, and clean monthly tax records.
